What the assistant can read

Positions and weights, cost basis, order and contribution history, performance and benchmark comparison, sealed forecasts and calibration, and the text of your memos and theses — for the accounts you select in the next step.

What it cannot do

The assistant cannot place, modify, or cancel an order. It cannot change an allocation, move capital, alter your Core portfolio, or write anything back to this account. Access is read-only. These functions are not exposed to any assistant and are not configurable.

No advice

Intelligent Investing does not supply the assistant with recommendations, target allocations, or return forecasts, and does not review what the assistant tells you. Output from a third-party assistant is not advice from Intelligent Investing and is not a suitability determination. Responsibility for security selection, position sizing, and every execution decision remains yours.

Record and revocation

Every read is recorded. Access can be revoked at any time under Connections; revocation ends future access and does not recall what was already read. Records are retained for seven years.
